Your Magic wallet shows all your digital assets in one place — tokens, NFTs, and your transaction history across every network you use. Everything is organized in a single dashboard so you never have to juggle multiple apps or browser extensions to see what you own.

## Viewing your token balances

Your total wallet balance is the first thing you see when you open the Magic dashboard. To drill into the details:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your Magic account">
    Go to your Magic dashboard and log in. Your wallet loads automatically — no extra steps needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your main balance">
    Your aggregate wallet balance appears at the top of the dashboard, displayed in your local currency. Individual token balances are listed below it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a network to see chain-specific assets">
    Click on a network name — such as **Ethereum**, **Polygon**, or **Solana** — to filter the asset list to only the tokens held on that chain. Each network displays its own token balances independently.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching between networks

Use the **network selector** in the top-right corner of the wallet view to switch between blockchains. Magic supports 30+ networks, including Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, and more. When you switch networks, your token list, NFT gallery, and activity feed all update to reflect that chain.

If you don't see a token you're expecting, confirm that you've selected the correct network — assets on one chain won't appear when you're viewing another.

## Viewing NFTs

Open the **NFTs** tab inside your wallet to browse any non-fungible tokens associated with your address. Each NFT displays its image, name, and the collection it belongs to. Tap or click any NFT to see its full metadata, including the token ID and the contract address it was minted on.

If an NFT isn't showing up, it may be on a different network — use the network selector to switch chains and check again.

## Transaction history

The **Activity** feed records every action your wallet has taken: tokens sent and received, NFT transfers, and interactions with decentralized apps. Each entry shows:

- The **type** of transaction (send, receive, contract interaction)
- The **amount** and asset involved
- The **date and time** it was confirmed
- The **status** — confirmed, pending, or failed

<Tip>
  Copy any transaction hash from the activity feed and paste it into a block explorer — like [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io) for Ethereum or [Solscan](https://solscan.io) for Solana — to see the full on-chain details, including the exact block it was included in.
</Tip>

<Info>
  Token prices shown in your dashboard are estimates pulled from market data feeds and may vary depending on the exchange or liquidity source. They are provided for reference only and do not represent a guaranteed trade price.
</Info>

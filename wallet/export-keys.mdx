Because Magic is non-custodial, you can export your private key at any time. This lets you use your wallet in any other compatible app — like MetaMask, Ledger, or any EVM-compatible wallet — without losing access to the assets you've already received.

<Warning>
  Never share your private key with anyone — not with Magic support, not with a friend, not on any website that asks for it. Anyone who has your private key has complete, irrevocable control over your wallet and every asset inside it. Magic will never ask you for your private key.
</Warning>

## What is a private key?

Your private key is the master credential for your wallet. It's a long string of letters and numbers — unique to your wallet — that mathematically proves you own the funds at your address. When you sign a transaction, you're using your private key to authorize it.

Unlike a password, a private key cannot be reset or recovered. If you lose it and lose access to your Magic account, there is no way to recover the funds in your wallet. This is why storing it safely is so important.

## How to export your key

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your Magic account">
    Open your Magic dashboard and log in using your usual method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Security">
    Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner and select **Settings**. Then open the **Security** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title='Click "Export Private Key"'>
    Scroll to the **Private Key** section and click the **Export Private Key** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    Magic will prompt you to re-authenticate — for example, by confirming an email code or completing your usual login flow. This step protects you in case someone else has access to your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and store your private key immediately">
    Your private key is displayed on screen for a limited time. Copy it right away and save it somewhere secure — such as a password manager (like 1Password or Bitwarden), or written down and stored in a physically secure location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to do with your exported key

Once you have your private key, you can import it into any compatible wallet application:

- **MetaMask** — Open MetaMask, click the account selector, and choose **Import account**. Paste your private key and click **Import**.
- **Ledger** — Use Ledger Live to import an existing account using your private key, or use the key to derive a compatible hardware wallet account.
- **Any EVM-compatible wallet** — Look for an **Import account**, **Import wallet**, or **Import private key** option in the wallet's settings. The process is the same across most apps.

Your imported wallet will have access to the same address and all the assets currently held there.

## Keeping your key safe

How you store your private key is just as important as exporting it correctly. Follow these practices to keep your funds secure:

- **Store it offline whenever possible.** A printed copy kept in a locked drawer or safe is far harder to steal than a digital file.
- **Use a hardware wallet for long-term storage.** Devices like a Ledger or Trezor keep your key isolated from internet-connected systems.
- **Never email your private key or send it over chat.** Email and messaging apps are common targets for attackers.
- **Never take a screenshot of your private key.** Screenshots sync to cloud services on most devices, creating unintended copies.
- **Don't save it in a plain text file on your computer.** If your device is compromised, plain text files are immediately readable by an attacker.
- **Use a reputable password manager** if you need a digital copy — these encrypt your data at rest.

<Note>
  Exporting your private key does not remove your wallet from Magic. Your Magic account and wallet continue to work exactly as before. You can use Magic and any external wallet simultaneously — they both point to the same underlying address.
</Note>

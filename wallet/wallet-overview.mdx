## Non-custodial: you own your keys

Magic is a **non-custodial** wallet. That means you — and only you — own the private keys that control your funds. Magic never holds, stores, or has access to your private key at any point.

This matters because it means:

- **No one can freeze your funds.** Magic cannot restrict access to your assets.
- **No one can move your money without your permission.** Every transaction requires your approval.
- **You're in control.** If you ever want to leave Magic, you can export your private key and use your wallet in any compatible app.

This is different from leaving money in a centralized exchange, where the platform holds your keys on your behalf. With Magic, the keys are yours from the start.

## Your wallet address

Your wallet address is a unique identifier — think of it like a bank account number for the blockchain. Anyone can send digital assets to your wallet address, and you can share it freely without risk.

To find your wallet address:

1. Sign in to your Magic account.
2. Open the **Wallet** tab in your dashboard.
3. Your address appears at the top of the wallet view. Click the copy icon to copy it to your clipboard.

<Note>
  Your wallet address is public — anyone can send funds to it, and anyone can look it up on a block explorer. Keep your **private key** completely secret. Sharing your address is safe; sharing your private key is not.
</Note>

## Multiple networks

Your Magic wallet works across more than 30 blockchains. Each network has its own separate balance and, in some cases, its own wallet address format. For example, your Ethereum address and your Solana address are different strings, even though they both belong to the same Magic account.

Use the network selector in your wallet dashboard to switch between chains and view the assets you hold on each one. For a full list of supported blockchains, see the [Supported Networks](/guides/supported-networks) guide.

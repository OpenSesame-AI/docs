When you use your Magic wallet in an app or website, that connection is recorded in your dashboard. You are always in control — you can review every app that has access to your wallet and remove any of them in seconds.

## Viewing Connected Apps

To see the full list of apps connected to your wallet, navigate to **Settings → Connected Apps** in your Magic dashboard. Each entry in the list shows:

- The name and logo of the app
- The date you first connected it
- The specific permissions it was granted at the time of connection

If you do not recognize an app in the list, treat it as a signal to revoke its access immediately and review any recent transaction activity in your [Activity Feed](/account/dashboard-overview#recent-activity).

## What App Permissions Mean

Permissions define what an app is allowed to do with your wallet. Magic uses a minimal-permission model, and no app can move funds without your explicit approval. In plain terms:

- **View wallet address** — The app can see your public wallet address. This is read-only and does not expose any funds.
- **Request transaction approvals** — The app can prompt you to approve or reject a transaction. You must confirm every request before anything is sent.
- **Read activity** — The app can view your transaction history associated with your wallet address.

No permission grants an app the ability to act on your wallet without you knowing. Every important action requires your confirmation inside the Magic interface.

<Warning>
  Revoking an app's access **does not undo or reverse past transactions**. If you approved a transaction while the app was connected, that transaction is permanently recorded on the blockchain. Revocation only prevents the app from making future requests to your wallet.
</Warning>

## Revoking an App's Access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings → Connected Apps">
    Open your Magic dashboard and navigate to **Settings**, then select **Connected Apps** from the sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the app you want to disconnect">
    Scroll through the list of connected apps or use the search bar to locate the specific app by name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Revoke Access">
    Select the app to expand its details, then click the **Revoke Access** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the action">
    A confirmation dialog will appear. Click **Confirm** to finalize the revocation. The app is immediately removed from your connected apps list and can no longer send requests to your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Safe App Practices

Staying safe when connecting your wallet to third-party apps comes down to a few consistent habits:

- **Only connect apps you trust.** Research an app before connecting your wallet. Look for a transparent team, positive user reviews, and an active community before giving access.
- **Check the URL before connecting.** Phishing sites mimic legitimate apps with near-identical domain names. Confirm the URL is exactly correct before approving any connection.
- **Watch for unusual transaction requests.** Legitimate apps only ask for approvals that match what you are trying to do. If an app requests an unexpected or unusually large approval, decline it and disconnect the app.
- **Review your connected apps periodically.** Make it a habit to check your Connected Apps list and remove anything you no longer use.

<Note>
  Magic **never shares your private key** with connected apps under any circumstances. Apps interact with your wallet only through Magic's approval system, which requires your explicit confirmation for every action that affects your funds.
</Note>

Creating a Magic account automatically creates your digital wallet — no password or seed phrase required. The entire process takes about two minutes, and once complete, your wallet is ready to use across 30+ blockchains without any additional setup.

## Sign Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to magic.link">
    Open [magic.link](https://magic.link) in your browser, or download the Magic app from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com). Both paths lead to the same account — use whichever is most convenient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email or pick a social login">
    Type your email address into the sign-up field, or choose a social provider such as Google, Apple, GitHub, Twitter/X, or Discord. Magic supports multiple social providers so you can use the account you already trust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    If you signed up with email, check your inbox for a one-time verification code. Enter the code on the confirmation screen to prove ownership of the address.

    <Note>
      The one-time code is valid for **10 minutes**. If it expires before you enter it, return to the sign-up screen and request a new one.
    </Note>

    If you chose a social login, you will be redirected to that provider's own sign-in screen. Approve the request there and you will be brought back to Magic automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your account is ready">
    You are now signed in. Magic immediately creates your wallet in the background — no extra steps, no seed phrase to write down, and no password to set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Gets Created

When your account is created, Magic generates a **personal wallet address** that belongs exclusively to you. This address is your identity in the world of digital assets — it is where you receive tokens, where sends originate from, and how apps recognize you.

You can find your wallet address at any time on the Wallet page of your dashboard. It is a unique string of letters and numbers and is safe to share with anyone who needs to send you assets.

Because your wallet is non-custodial, Magic never holds your funds on your behalf. You remain the sole owner at all times.

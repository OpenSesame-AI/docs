## Wallet Summary

At the top of the dashboard you will find your **wallet summary**. This panel displays your total portfolio value in your chosen currency (for example, USD or EUR), followed by a breakdown of each individual asset you hold. Each asset row shows the token name, the quantity you own, and its current market value.

Click any asset in the list to open its detail view, where you can initiate a send, copy your receive address, or view the full transaction history for that specific token.

## Recent Activity

Below the wallet summary, the **Recent Activity** panel shows a scrollable timeline of your transaction history. Each entry includes:

- The transaction type (send, receive, or app interaction)
- The amount and asset involved
- The date and time
- A link to view the transaction on the relevant blockchain's public record

The feed updates automatically — refresh the page or pull down in the mobile app to load the latest entries. Transactions typically appear within a few seconds of being confirmed.

## Account Settings

The **Settings** section gives you control over the personal details and security options tied to your Magic account. From here you can:

- Change your display name shown in the dashboard
- Manage the email addresses and social accounts linked to your Magic login
- View security settings, including active sessions and sign-in history
- Export your private key to move your wallet to another provider if you ever need to

<Tip>
  Install the Magic mobile app for on-the-go access to your dashboard. Every section available on desktop — including wallet management, activity, and settings — is fully accessible from your phone.
</Tip>

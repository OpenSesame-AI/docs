Magic's email login sends a one-time verification code to your inbox each time you sign in. There's no password to create, remember, or reset — just your email address and a quick code that proves it's you.

## How email sign-in works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    On the Magic sign-in screen, type your email address and click **Continue**. Make sure you use an address you have access to right now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    Magic sends a 6-digit code to that address within a few seconds. Look for an email with the subject line **"Your Magic sign-in code"**. If it doesn't appear right away, give it a moment and check your spam folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Type the 6-digit code on the Magic sign-in screen and click **Verify**. That's it — you're signed in and your wallet is ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Sign-in codes expire after **10 minutes**. If your code has expired before you enter it, click **Resend code** on the sign-in screen to get a fresh one.
</Note>

## Didn't receive the code?

If the email hasn't arrived after a minute or two, work through the steps below.

<Accordion title="Check your spam folder">
  Email providers occasionally filter automated messages. Open your spam or junk folder and look for an email from Magic. If you find it there, mark it as **Not Spam** so future codes land in your inbox.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Make sure you typed the right email">
  Go back to the sign-in screen and double-check the address you entered. A single mistyped character means the code went somewhere else. Correct any typos and click **Continue** again to request a new code.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Request a new code">
  On the code-entry screen, click **Resend code**. Magic will send a fresh 6-digit code to your email address. Previous codes are immediately invalidated when you resend, so only use the newest one.
</Accordion>

## Changing your email address

You can update the email address linked to your Magic account at any time. Go to **Settings → Account**, find the **Email address** field, and enter your new address. Magic will send a verification code to the new address to confirm you own it before saving the change.

<Tip>
  Prefer to sign in with one tap instead of entering a code? You can use Google, Apple, or another social account instead. See [Social Login](/auth/social-login) to get started.
</Tip>

Social login lets you use an account you already have — like Google or Apple — to sign in to Magic instantly. There's nothing new to remember: you simply confirm with a provider you already trust, and Magic takes care of the rest.

## Supported providers

Magic supports sign-in through all of the following providers. Click any one of them on the sign-in screen to get started.

## How to sign in with a social account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Magic sign-in screen">
    Go to [magic.link](https://magic.link) or open any Magic-powered app. You'll see a set of social login buttons alongside the email option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your provider">
    Click the icon for the account you want to use — for example, **Continue with Google**. You'll be redirected to that provider's login page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Magic">
    Your provider will ask you to confirm that you want to give Magic access to your basic profile information. Click **Allow** or **Continue** to proceed. Magic only uses this to identify you — it never sees your social account password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    Magic creates or reopens your account in the background. You're signed in, and your wallet is ready to use — no extra steps required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each social provider is tied to a **separate Magic wallet**. If you signed up with Google, always sign back in with Google to reach the same wallet. Switching providers won't merge your accounts.
</Note>

## Linking multiple providers

You don't have to choose just one sign-in method. Once you're signed in, go to **Settings → Account** and find the **Linked accounts** section. From there you can connect additional social providers or add an email address to your account. Every linked method gives you another way to access the **same wallet** — so you're covered even if you lose access to one provider.

<Warning>
  Signing in with a different provider than the one you used at signup will create a **brand-new, separate wallet** — your previous wallet and its contents won't be visible. Always use the same sign-in method you originally used, unless you've explicitly linked another provider in Settings.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Want the fastest sign-in experience with no redirects at all? Set up a passkey and sign in with just your face or fingerprint. See [Passkeys](/auth/passkeys) to learn how.
</Tip>

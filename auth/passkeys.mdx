Passkeys let you sign in to Magic using your device's built-in security — like Face ID, Touch ID, or a fingerprint scanner — instead of typing a code or password. Once you set one up, signing in takes a single biometric confirmation.

## What is a passkey?

A passkey is a secure digital key that your device generates and stores for you. When you sign in, your device uses that key to confirm your identity — without ever sending a password or secret across the internet. Even if someone intercepts the sign-in request, there's nothing useful for them to steal. Passkeys are the same technology used to secure online banking and device unlock screens, and they are fully resistant to phishing by design.

## Setting up a passkey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to Magic">
    Use your existing sign-in method — email code or a social account — to access your Magic account. You need to be signed in before you can register a passkey.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Security">
    Tap or click your profile avatar to open the menu, then navigate to **Settings → Security**. Scroll down to the **Passkeys** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a passkey">
    Click **Add passkey**. Your browser or operating system will prompt you to authenticate using Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or your device PIN. Follow the on-screen prompt to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once your biometric or PIN is verified, the passkey is saved and linked to your Magic account. The next time you sign in, you can select **Use a passkey** and authenticate in one step — no codes, no passwords.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your passkey is stored **on your device** — not on Magic's servers. On Apple devices it syncs securely through iCloud Keychain; on Android it syncs through Google Password Manager. Magic never has access to the key itself.
</Note>

## Signing in with a passkey

Once a passkey is registered, the sign-in flow is just two steps:

1. **Open the Magic sign-in screen** — go to [magic.link](https://magic.link) or open a Magic-powered app and click **Use a passkey**.
2. **Confirm with your biometrics** — your device will prompt you for Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or PIN. Approve it, and you're instantly signed in.

<Tip>
  You can register passkeys on **multiple devices** — for example, your phone and your laptop — so you're never locked out when you switch between them. Repeat the setup steps above on each device you want to use.
</Tip>

## Removing a passkey

To delete a passkey you no longer need, go to **Settings → Security → Passkeys**. Find the passkey you want to remove (they're labeled by device name and registration date), click the three-dot menu next to it, and select **Remove**. The passkey is immediately deauthorized.

<Warning>
  If you remove all your passkeys **and** lose access to your sign-in email or social accounts, you will be locked out of your Magic wallet with no recovery path. Always keep at least one other verified sign-in method — such as your email address or a linked social account — active in **Settings → Account** before removing a passkey.
</Warning>

## How it works

Getting started with Magic takes less than five minutes and follows three straightforward steps. You don't need to install browser extensions, write down recovery phrases, or understand how blockchains work under the hood.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [magic.link](https://magic.link) and click **Get Started**. Enter your email address or choose a social login — that's all you need to create your Magic account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in your way">
    Magic sends a one-time code to your email, or authenticates you through your chosen social provider. No password to remember, no authenticator app required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start using your wallet">
    Your personal wallet is created automatically the moment you sign in. You can receive assets, view balances across multiple networks, and send funds right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Magic is **non-custodial** — you always own your private keys. Magic never has access to your funds, and you can export your keys at any time to use with any compatible wallet app.
</Note>

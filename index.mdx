Edward is your brokerage's AI underwriting assistant. Ask an underwriting question in plain language (coverage eligibility, carrier appetite, surcharges, exclusions) and Edward answers in seconds, with the carrier rule **cited back to the source document**. Every answer is grounded in your carriers' own manuals and policy documents, never in guesswork.

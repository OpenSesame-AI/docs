Magic is a digital wallet platform that lets you create, access, and use a blockchain wallet using nothing more than your email address or a social account you already have. Think of it as a secure vault for your digital assets — one that you unlock with tools you already use rather than a 24-word recovery phrase that's easy to misplace and impossible to guess. Behind the scenes, Magic uses industry-standard cryptography to protect your wallet; on the surface, it feels as simple as logging into any website.

## Your wallet, your keys

When people talk about "owning" a crypto wallet, they mean owning the private key — the secret piece of information that proves assets belong to you and authorizes every transaction. With many wallet services, a company holds that key on your behalf, which means you're trusting them to keep it safe and to give it back when you ask.

Magic works differently. Your private key is generated and encrypted on your own device. Magic's servers never see it, never store it, and never have the ability to move your assets. Your wallet is genuinely yours — not an account that a company manages for you.

<Note>
  Because Magic never holds your private key, no one at Magic can freeze your wallet, reverse a transaction, or access your funds. The tradeoff is that keeping your sign-in method (your email or social account) secure is important — that's your path back in if you switch devices.
</Note>

## No seed phrases

Traditional wallets give you a seed phrase — a list of 12 to 24 random words — when you first set up the wallet. If you lose those words, you lose your wallet forever. If someone else finds them, they own everything inside.

Magic eliminates seed phrases entirely. Instead of tying your wallet access to a static list of words, Magic uses your verified identity as the access layer. Sign in with your email one-time code, your Google account, your Apple ID, GitHub, Twitter/X, Discord, or a passkey (biometric sign-in using Face ID or a fingerprint reader). Your key is reconstructed securely during sign-in and never written to a file you could accidentally share or delete.

## Which blockchains does Magic support?

Magic works across a wide range of blockchain networks, so you're not locked into one ecosystem. Supported networks include:

- **Ethereum** — the most widely used smart contract network
- **Polygon** — fast and low-cost transactions
- **Arbitrum** — Ethereum layer-2 scaling
- **Base** — Coinbase's layer-2 network
- **Solana** — high-speed, low-fee transactions
- **25+ additional networks** — including BNB Chain, Optimism, Avalanche, and more

You can switch between networks from within your wallet at any time without creating separate accounts.

## Is Magic free to use?

For everyday users, Magic is free to access. Creating an account, signing in, and viewing your wallet costs nothing. Like all blockchain activity, sending assets requires a small network fee (often called "gas") paid to the blockchain itself — this is not a Magic fee and varies by network and transaction type. Magic does not charge you a percentage of your assets or a monthly subscription to use your wallet.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordion title="Do I own my wallet?">
  Yes — completely. Your private keys are encrypted and stored on your device, not on Magic's servers. No one at Magic can access, freeze, or move your assets. You are the sole owner of your wallet and everything inside it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if I lose access to my email?">
  Your email (or social account) is your key to signing back into your wallet from a new device. If you lose access to your email, recover that email account first through your email provider's standard account recovery process. Once you regain access to your email, you can sign back into Magic and your wallet will be waiting. It's a good idea to link a backup social account — such as Google or Apple — while you still have access, giving yourself a second recovery path.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Magic on mobile?">
  Yes. Magic works on iOS and Android through both the dedicated Magic app and through any mobile browser. Your wallet is the same regardless of which device you sign in from — switch between your phone, tablet, and desktop freely, and your assets and history stay in sync.
</Accordion>

You don't need to be technical to get started with Magic. From the moment you land on the Magic website to the moment your wallet is ready to use, the whole process takes under five minutes — and at no point will you be asked to install a browser extension, write down a seed phrase, or understand how blockchains work. All you need is an email address or a social account you already use.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit magic.link">
    Open your browser and go to [magic.link](https://magic.link), or download the Magic app from the App Store or Google Play. On the homepage, click **Get Started** to begin creating your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter your email address, or choose one of the available sign-in options — Google, Apple, GitHub, Twitter/X, or Discord. If you use email, Magic immediately sends a one-time verification code to your inbox. Enter that code to confirm your identity. There's no password to create or remember — the code is your sign-in, and a fresh one is generated each time you log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your wallet">
    Once you're signed in, Magic automatically creates your personal wallet. You'll land on your wallet dashboard, where you can see your wallet address, view your asset balances, and explore the networks Magic supports. Share your wallet address with anyone to start receiving funds, or click **Receive** to get a scannable QR code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your email account secure and accessible — it's your primary way to recover access to your Magic wallet if you sign in on a new device. Consider linking a social account as a backup while you're getting set up.
</Tip>

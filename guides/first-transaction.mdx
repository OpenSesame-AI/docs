Sending your first transaction is a big milestone. This guide walks you through the whole process — from having a funded wallet to completing a successful send — so you can move digital assets with confidence.

## Before you begin

<Note>
  Make sure you have the following ready before you start:

  - Your Magic account is fully set up and you can sign in
  - Your wallet has funds available — if not, see [Add Funds](/guides/add-funds) first
  - You have the recipient's wallet address copied and ready to paste
</Note>

## Step-by-step: your first send

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Magic wallet">
    Sign in to Magic using your email, social login, or passkey. Once authenticated, you land on your wallet dashboard where you can see your balance and recent activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send">
    Find the **Send** button on your wallet dashboard — it's typically displayed prominently near your balance. Click it to open the send flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the recipient address">
    Paste the wallet address you want to send to into the recipient field. Triple-check every character before continuing — blockchain transactions cannot be reversed or cancelled once submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the asset and amount">
    Select the token you want to send from the asset dropdown (for example, ETH or USDC), then type in the amount. Magic shows you your available balance so you don't accidentally overspend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review fees">
    Magic displays the estimated network fee alongside your send amount. Confirm you have enough to cover **both** the amount you're sending and the fee — if not, reduce the amount or top up your wallet first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Confirm** to sign and broadcast the transaction to the blockchain. Magic handles the cryptographic signing behind the scenes — no seed phrase required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Your transaction is now submitted to the network. You'll see it appear in your activity feed with a **Pending** status. Once the network processes it — usually within seconds to a couple of minutes — the status updates to **Confirmed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you send funds to the wrong address, they are gone permanently. There is no undo, no customer support reversal, and no way to recover assets sent to an incorrect address on the blockchain. Always verify the recipient address before clicking Confirm.
</Warning>

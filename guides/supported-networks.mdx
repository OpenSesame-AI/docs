## Switching networks

You can switch the active network in your Magic wallet at any time without creating a new account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Magic wallet">
    Sign in and go to your wallet dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the network name">
    Find the network indicator near your balance — it shows the name of whichever chain you're currently on. Click it to open the network selector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the network you want">
    Choose your target network from the list. Your balance and activity feed will update to reflect that network immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your wallet address may look different on networks like Solana or Flow compared to Ethereum-based networks — this is completely normal. All Ethereum-compatible networks (Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, and so on) share the same address format, so your address will be identical across all of them.

<Note>
  Each network uses its own token to pay for transactions. Make sure you hold a small amount of that token before sending or interacting with apps. For example, you need ETH to pay fees on Ethereum, MATIC on Polygon, and SOL on Solana. Without the correct fee token, transactions will fail.
</Note>

## Which network should I use?

Not sure where to start? Use the questions below to find the right fit.

<Accordion title="I want to use trading or finance apps">
  Start with **Ethereum** for access to the most established trading apps (Uniswap, Aave, Compound, and more), or try **Arbitrum** for the same ecosystem with significantly lower transaction fees. Most major apps are available on both.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want low fees">
  **Polygon**, **Base**, and **Arbitrum** all offer transaction fees that are a fraction of Ethereum costs. Polygon is especially beginner-friendly for low-cost experimentation and has broad app support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want to use Solana apps">
  Select **Solana** from the network switcher. Solana has its own thriving ecosystem of trading apps, digital art marketplaces, and games that are separate from Ethereum-based networks. Your Solana wallet address is unique to that network.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I'm not sure where to start">
  Begin with **Polygon**. It is fully compatible with Ethereum (so everything you learn transfers directly), transaction fees are very low, and there are plenty of apps to explore without spending much. Once you're comfortable, branching out to other networks is straightforward.
</Accordion>

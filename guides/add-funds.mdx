There are two ways to add funds to your Magic wallet — buy crypto directly using a debit or credit card, or transfer from an exchange or another wallet you already own. Both methods are straightforward, and you can use whichever fits your situation.

## Option 1: Buy crypto with a card (fiat on-ramp)

This is the quickest route if you don't already hold crypto elsewhere. Magic's built-in on-ramp lets you purchase digital assets with a standard payment card in just a few steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your Magic wallet">
    Sign in to Magic and go to your wallet dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Add Funds' or 'Buy'">
    Look for the **Add Funds** or **Buy** button near your balance. Click it to open the on-ramp flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the crypto you want to buy">
    Select the asset you'd like to purchase — for example, ETH, MATIC, or SOL. If you're unsure which to choose, check [Supported Networks](/guides/supported-networks) to see what works best for your goals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount in your local currency">
    Type in how much you want to spend in your local currency (for example, USD or EUR). Magic shows you how much crypto you'll receive at the current rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the payment with your card">
    Enter your card details and finish the checkout. Magic's on-ramp partner processes the payment securely using industry-standard encryption.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for funds to arrive">
    Your purchased crypto typically appears in your Magic wallet within a few minutes. You'll see the balance update on your dashboard once the transaction confirms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Supported payment methods and available currencies may vary depending on your region. If a particular currency or card type isn't available, try the transfer method below.
</Info>

## Option 2: Transfer from an exchange or wallet

If you already hold crypto on an exchange like Coinbase or Binance, or in another wallet, you can send it directly to your Magic wallet address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy your Magic wallet address">
    In your Magic dashboard, navigate to **Receive** and copy your wallet address. Double-check you're on the correct network before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to your exchange or other wallet">
    Open the platform where your funds currently live and go to the withdrawal or send section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate a withdrawal to your Magic address">
    Paste your copied Magic wallet address into the recipient field. Take care not to edit the address after pasting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the correct network">
    Choose the network that matches your Magic wallet. For example, select **Ethereum mainnet** when sending ETH. Sending on a mismatched network is one of the most common causes of lost funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and wait for the transfer">
    Submit the withdrawal on your exchange or wallet and wait for the transfer to arrive. Check your Magic activity feed — the incoming transaction will appear there once the network picks it up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Always send on the correct network. Sending ETH over BSC instead of Ethereum mainnet, for example, can result in permanently lost funds. When in doubt, send a small test amount first before transferring a larger sum.
</Warning>

## How long does it take?

On-ramp purchases through the card flow typically complete within **1–5 minutes** after your payment is approved. Blockchain transfers depend on the network you're using — most modern networks settle in seconds to a couple of minutes, though Ethereum mainnet can occasionally take longer during periods of high congestion.

<Tip>
  New to crypto? Start with a small amount while you get comfortable with how wallets and transfers work. There's no minimum — even a few dollars is enough to explore.
</Tip>

Your Magic wallet works with thousands of apps across the web — from trading platforms and digital art marketplaces to blockchain games and social communities. Every connection goes through Magic, so you are always in control of what each app can access and do on your behalf.

## How app connections work

When you visit a Web3 app and click **Connect Wallet**, the site requests permission to read your wallet address. That's all an initial connection does — it lets the app know who you are so it can show you your balances, collectibles, or other relevant data.

Sending funds, swapping tokens, creating NFTs, or signing any data are separate actions. Each one triggers a dedicated approval prompt inside Magic, which you must explicitly confirm. No app can move your funds or sign transactions silently in the background.

## Connecting your wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit a supported app">
    Navigate to the Web3 app you want to use — for example, a trading platform or digital art marketplace. Make sure you're on the genuine site URL (see the safety tip below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Connect Wallet'">
    Find the **Connect Wallet** button, usually in the top-right corner of the site, and click it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Magic from the wallet options">
    Select **Magic** from the list of wallet providers. If Magic isn't listed by name, look for **WalletConnect** — it works with Magic on most apps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the connection in Magic">
    A Magic prompt appears asking you to approve the connection request. Review the app name and domain shown in the prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Approve">
    Click **Approve** to complete the connection. The app can now see your wallet address and display your on-chain data. It cannot spend your funds without a separate, explicit confirmation from you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approving a transaction

Once connected, interacting with an app — swapping tokens, buying a digital collectible, or earning rewards — will trigger a transaction that needs your sign-off inside Magic.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Initiate an action in the app">
    Perform an action in the app that requires a transaction, such as swapping tokens, buying a digital collectible, or earning rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Magic approval popup">
    A Magic approval popup appears automatically, showing you the full details of what you're being asked to approve — including the transaction type, amounts involved, and the receiving address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the details carefully">
    Review the **amount**, the **destination address**, and the **estimated network fee** before proceeding. If anything looks unfamiliar or unexpectedly large, reject it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm or reject">
    Click **Confirm** to submit the transaction, or **Reject** to cancel it entirely. Rejecting a transaction does not disconnect you from the app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Read every transaction request carefully before confirming. Malicious apps and phishing sites can present deceptive prompts designed to drain your wallet. If a request asks you to approve an unusually large amount, transfer all your assets, or interact with an unrecognized address, reject it immediately.
</Warning>

## Managing connected apps

You can review and revoke app connections at any time from your Magic account settings. Visit [Manage Connected Apps](/account/manage-apps) to see which apps have access to your wallet address and disconnect any you no longer use or recognize.

<Tip>
  Only connect to apps with a URL you've verified as genuine. Bookmark the sites you use regularly — it's the easiest way to avoid accidentally landing on a phishing copy with a slightly different domain.
</Tip>

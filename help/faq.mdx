Here you'll find answers to the questions users ask most often about Magic. If something isn't covered below, visit our [troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting) or [contact support](/help/contact).

## Account & Sign-In

<Accordion title="Do I need a password to use Magic?">
  No. Magic replaces passwords entirely. Instead, you sign in using a one-time code sent to your email, a social account (Google, Apple, GitHub, X, or Discord), or a passkey stored on your device. There's nothing to memorize and nothing to forget.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I lose access to my email?">
  You can link multiple sign-in methods to a single Magic account before you need them. Go to **Settings → Account** and add a social account or passkey as a backup. If you've already exported your private key, you can import it into any compatible wallet and continue accessing your assets independently of your email.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Magic on my phone?">
  Yes. Magic has a mobile app available for both iOS and Android. Magic also works directly in your mobile browser, so you can access your wallet without installing anything.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I have multiple wallets?">
  Each sign-in method — for example, your email address and your Google account — creates its own separate wallet by default. To manage all your assets from one place, link your sign-in methods together in **Settings → Account**. Linked methods share a single unified wallet.
</Accordion>

## Wallet & Assets

<Accordion title="Can Magic see or access my wallet?">
  No. Magic is non-custodial, which means your private key is generated on your device and is never stored on Magic's servers. Magic cannot view your balance, access your funds, freeze your account, or move your assets. You are always in full control.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who controls my private key?">
  You do. Your private key never leaves your device and is never shared with Magic. You can export it at any time from **Settings → Security** and import it into any compatible wallet. Your assets stay with your key — not with Magic.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use my Magic wallet in other apps like MetaMask?">
  Yes. Go to **Settings → Security** and export your private key. You can then import that key into MetaMask, Rabby, or any other compatible wallet. Your assets move with the key — not with Magic.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are my funds insured?">
  Magic does not provide insurance on wallet funds. Assets stored in your wallet are your responsibility. Magic recommends keeping an offline backup of your private key and following the security best practices described in our [security guide](/help/security).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are network fees?">
  Network fees are small charges paid directly to the blockchain network to process your transactions — similar to a processing fee. Magic does not collect or profit from these fees. The exact amount depends on which network you're using and how busy it is at the time of your transaction.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I lose funds if Magic shuts down?">
  No. Because Magic is non-custodial and you can export your private key at any time, your access to your wallet does not depend on Magic's services being online. Import your key into any compatible wallet and your funds remain fully accessible.
</Accordion>

## Networks & Buying Crypto

<Accordion title="Which blockchains and networks does Magic support?">
  Magic supports over 30 blockchains, including Ethereum and all Ethereum-compatible networks (such as Polygon, Base, BNB Chain, and Avalanche), as well as Solana and Bitcoin. You can switch between networks inside your wallet at any time. If you're not sure which network to use, the app or service you're working with will usually tell you.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I buy crypto directly in Magic?">
  Yes. Magic includes a built-in way to purchase cryptocurrency using a credit or debit card — no separate exchange account needed. Look for the **Buy** option inside your wallet to get started. Availability may vary depending on your country and the asset you want to purchase.
</Accordion>

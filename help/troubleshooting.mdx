Most issues with Magic can be resolved quickly. This page covers the most common problems and how to fix them. Work through the steps for your issue in order — the first step resolves the problem most of the time.

## Sign-In Issues

<Accordion title="I'm not receiving my email sign-in code">
  Follow these steps in order:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check your spam or junk folder">
      Email providers occasionally filter one-time code messages. Search your inbox for "Magic" or "magic.link" to locate it.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm you're using the correct email address">
      Make sure you typed the same email you originally signed up with. A single typo routes the code to the wrong inbox.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Click Resend Code and wait">
      Click the **Resend Code** button on the sign-in screen. Wait at least 60 seconds between attempts — sending too many requests in quick succession can cause temporary delays.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Try a different browser or incognito mode">
      Browser extensions (particularly ad blockers and privacy tools) can sometimes interfere with the sign-in flow. Open an incognito or private window and try again.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  If you've completed all four steps and still haven't received a code, [contact support](/help/contact) with your account email and the browser you're using.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My sign-in code expired">
  Sign-in codes expire after **10 minutes** for security reasons. Click **Resend code** on the sign-in screen to generate a fresh one, and complete sign-in promptly after it arrives. If your email is slow to deliver, switching to a passkey or social login eliminates expiry issues entirely — you can add one in **Settings → Security**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't sign in with my social account">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Confirm you're using the same provider you signed up with">
      A Google account and an Apple account create separate wallets. Make sure you're clicking the same provider you originally used.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Clear cookies or switch browsers">
      Saved login data from your social provider can sometimes block a fresh sign-in. Clear your browser cookies or open an incognito window and try again.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check permissions in your social provider's settings">
      Go to your Google or Apple account's connected apps page and verify that Magic still has permission to sign you in. If you previously removed it, re-authorize it and try again.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

## Lost Access to Email or Account Recovery

<Accordion title="I've lost access to the email address I used to sign up">
  If you can no longer access your sign-in email, try these options in order:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Sign in with a linked social account or passkey">
      If you previously added a social login (Google, Apple, GitHub, X, or Discord) or a passkey as a backup, use that to sign in instead. Go to the Magic sign-in screen and choose the alternative method.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Recover your email account first">
      Contact your email provider's support team to regain access to your original email address. Once recovered, you can sign back in to Magic normally.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Use your exported private key">
      If you previously exported your private key from **Settings → Security**, you can import it into any compatible wallet (such as MetaMask or Rabby) to access your assets directly — no Magic sign-in needed.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Contact Magic support">
      If none of the above options work, reach out to our team at [support@magic.link](mailto:support@magic.link). Provide as much account information as you can to help us verify your identity.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I add a backup sign-in method?">
  Go to **Settings → Account** while you're signed in and add a social account (Google, Apple, GitHub, X, or Discord) or a passkey. Doing this before you ever lose access to your primary sign-in method is the best way to protect yourself. You can add as many backup methods as you like.
</Accordion>

## Wallet & Balance Issues

<Accordion title="My balance shows zero but I sent funds">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Verify you're on the correct network">
      Your wallet displays balances per network. Use the network selector at the top of your wallet to switch to the network you sent funds on (for example, Ethereum, Polygon, or Base).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Wait for the transaction to finish">
      Transfers are not instant. Depending on how busy the network is, a transaction can take anywhere from a few seconds to several minutes to complete. Give it a little time, then refresh your wallet.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm the funds arrived at your address">
      Check your transaction history inside the wallet. If you see the transfer listed as completed, the funds should appear in your balance. If it still shows as in progress, wait a little longer and refresh again.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My transaction is stuck as 'Pending'">
  A transaction can get stuck when the network is very busy or when the network fee was set too low. Here's what to do:

  - Check your transaction history inside the wallet to see the current status.
  - If the transaction is genuinely stuck, wait — it will eventually fail on its own and your funds will return to your wallet automatically.
  - For future transactions, let Magic estimate the network fee automatically to avoid this happening again.

  Once a transaction has been submitted, Magic cannot cancel or speed it up — this is a limitation of how blockchain networks work.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I sent funds to the wrong address">
  Unfortunately, transfers on a blockchain cannot be reversed once they are complete. Once a transaction finishes, neither Magic nor anyone else can undo it or recover those funds. Always double-check the recipient address before confirming any transfer — copy and paste it directly rather than typing manually, and verify the beginning and end of the address match exactly.
</Accordion>

## App & Connection Issues

<Accordion title="An app can't find my Magic wallet">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Refresh the app's page">
      A simple page refresh resolves most connection issues. After refreshing, look for the wallet connection prompt again.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Switch to a supported browser">
      Chrome and Firefox provide the most reliable experience. If you're using a less common browser, switch and try again.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm Magic appears as a wallet option">
      Some apps list supported wallets by name. Look for Magic in the wallet selection screen. If it isn't listed, the app may not yet support Magic — check the app's help pages for information on supported wallets.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A transaction was rejected unexpectedly">
  Two causes account for most unexpected rejections:

  - **Not enough funds to cover the network fee.** Your wallet needs a small amount of the network's native currency (for example, ETH on Ethereum) to pay the processing fee, even when you're sending other types of tokens. Check your balance and add more of the native currency if needed.
  - **Transaction limit reached.** Some apps place a cap on how much you can transact in a single step. Try a smaller amount, or check the app's help pages for its per-transaction limits.

  If neither of these applies, copy the error message and [contact support](/help/contact) with the details.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  If your issue isn't listed here or the steps above didn't resolve it, our support team is ready to help. Visit the [Contact page](/help/contact) to reach us by live chat, email, or the community forum.
</Tip>

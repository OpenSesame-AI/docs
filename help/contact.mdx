Magic's support team is here to help. Whether you have a question, hit a technical issue, or just want to give feedback, here's how to reach us. Picking the right channel gets you a faster response.

## Support Channels

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Live Chat" icon="message">
    Available on [magic.link](https://magic.link). Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of any page. This is the fastest channel for urgent issues and typically connects you with an agent within minutes during business hours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email Support" icon="envelope">
    Send a detailed message to **support@magic.link**. Include as much context as possible (see the checklist below). We reply within 1 business day.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community Discord" icon="users">
    Ask questions and get answers from the Magic community at [magic.link/discord](https://magic.link/discord). Great for general how-to questions, tips, and connecting with other Magic users.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Help Center" icon="circle-check">
    Browse self-service articles and guides at [help.magic.link](https://help.magic.link). Many common questions are answered there and you can find step-by-step walkthroughs for the most popular tasks.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Before You Contact Support

Checking these resources first often resolves your issue immediately — and if it doesn't, you'll have the information our team needs ready to go.

<Tip>
  Before opening a support request, take a moment to:

  - Check the **[FAQ](/help/faq)** for answers to the most common questions about accounts, wallets, and fees.
  - Check the **[Troubleshooting guide](/help/troubleshooting)** for step-by-step fixes for sign-in issues, missing balances, and failed transactions.
  - Have your **wallet address** ready to share (your public address — never your private key).
  - Write down the **exact steps** that led to the issue so our team can reproduce it quickly.
</Tip>

## What to Include in Your Support Request

The more detail you provide upfront, the faster we can help. Include the following in every support request:

- **Your Magic account email** — the address you use to sign in
- **What you were trying to do** — for example, "I was trying to send funds to a friend"
- **What happened instead** — for example, "The transfer showed as in progress for over an hour and then disappeared"
- **Any error messages you saw** — copy the exact text or attach a screenshot
- **The network or blockchain involved** — for example, Ethereum, Polygon, Base, or Solana

<Warning>
  Magic support will **NEVER** ask for your private key or seed phrase — by email, chat, phone, or any other channel. If anyone claiming to be Magic support asks for this information, it is a scam. End the conversation immediately and report it to **support@magic.link**.
</Warning>

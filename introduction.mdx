Magic is a wallet platform that removes every barrier between you and the world of digital assets. Traditional crypto wallets ask you to memorize long seed phrases, manage private keys, and understand technical concepts before you can do anything useful. Magic replaces all of that with sign-in methods you already know — your email address, Google account, or Apple ID — so you can own and use a digital wallet without any prior blockchain experience.

## What you can do with Magic

Magic gives you a full-featured digital wallet wrapped in an experience that feels like any other app you use every day.

## Who Magic is for

Magic is built for anyone who wants to participate in the digital economy without becoming a blockchain expert first. If you've ever felt intimidated by crypto wallets, confused by seed phrases, or worried about making an irreversible mistake, Magic is designed specifically with you in mind. It's equally useful for someone receiving digital assets for the first time and for a power user who simply wants a cleaner, faster sign-in experience.

## How Magic keeps you safe

Security is built into every layer of Magic, and it doesn't require you to do anything complicated to benefit from it.

- **Your keys, your wallet.** Your private keys are generated on your device and never transmitted to Magic's servers. You are always the sole owner of your wallet.
- **No seed phrases to lose.** Magic replaces seed phrases with secure key management tied to your verified identity, eliminating the most common way people permanently lose access to their wallets.
- **Flexible, familiar sign-in.** Sign in using email one-time codes, Google, Apple, GitHub, Twitter/X, Discord, and more. You can also enable passkeys for biometric sign-in with Face ID or a fingerprint reader on supported devices.

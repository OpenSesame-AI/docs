Edward is your brokerage's AI underwriting assistant. Your team asks underwriting questions in plain language (coverage eligibility, carrier appetite, surcharges, exclusions) and Edward answers in seconds, with the carrier rule **cited back to the source document**. Every answer is grounded in your carriers' own manuals and policy documents, never in guesswork.

This guide is for **brokerage admins**: the person who manages the workspace, controls who can access it, and keeps the document library current.

## How onboarding works

Getting started with Edward is **white-glove**. Your General Magic contact does the heavy setup for you, so you don't touch any technical configuration:

<Steps>
  <Step title="You share your documents and carrier access">
    You provide your carrier underwriting manuals and policy documents, and the carrier access needed to keep them current.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We build your workspace">
    We create your brokerage workspace, set up your admin account, and ingest and verify your documents so they're ready to search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You take over the day-to-day">
    Once your workspace is live, you invite your team, control what each person can see, and manage your document library from the admin dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There's no self-serve signup. Your General Magic contact provisions your workspace and hands you an admin login. If you don't have one yet, reach out to your contact to get started.
</Note>

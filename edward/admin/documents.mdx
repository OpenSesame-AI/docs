Edward is only as good as the documents behind it. As an admin, you keep your brokerage's library accurate, correctly scoped, and up to date.

## Document types

Edward treats documents differently depending on how authoritative they are:

| Type | What it is | How Edward uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Manuals** | Carrier underwriting manuals | The primary, authoritative source. Underwriting answers are built from these. |
| **Supplementary** | Bulletins, payment plans, policy wordings | Updates and additions that modify the manual. For example, a newer bulletin overrides an older rule. |
| **General** | Brokerage-specific documents with no carrier, product, or region | Background context. Can add to an answer but never overrides an underwriting rule. |

<Info>
  Edward ranks evidence: manuals earn trust, supplementary documents refine it, and general documents only add context. An answer will never let a general document override a carrier's underwriting rule.
</Info>

## Where documents come from

Your workspace draws on two sources:

- **General Magic documents:** shared carrier documents we maintain, available to every brokerage and filtered to each broker's scopes.
- **Your brokerage's documents:** documents you upload, visible only within your brokerage and filtered to each broker's scopes.

## Adding a document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the file">
    On the **Documents** screen, click **Upload** and choose the PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm its details">
    Edward reads the document and proposes its carrier, product, region, and date. Review these, because accurate tagging is what routes the document to the right questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify it">
    Set the document's **Review** to **Verified**. Once its **Retrieval** shows **Ready**, it's live and Edward can answer from it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A document is live only when its **Review** is **Verified** and its **Retrieval** is **Ready**. If a broker says a document "isn't showing up" in answers, check those two columns on the **Documents** screen.
</Warning>

## Keeping your library current

Edward resolves conflicts by date: when two documents disagree, **the newer authority governs**. To keep answers accurate:

- Upload **bulletins and updates** as they arrive rather than editing old files. Edward layers the newer document on top of the base manual automatically.
- Replace outdated manuals when a carrier issues a new edition.
- Remove or re-scope documents that no longer apply.

<Tip>
  Duplicate uploads are handled automatically: if you upload a file that's already in the system, Edward recognizes it and won't reprocess it.
</Tip>
